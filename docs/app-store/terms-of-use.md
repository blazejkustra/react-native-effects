# Terms of use

No custom terms are needed for version 1.0.0. The app is free and has no accounts, purchases, subscriptions or user-generated content, so Apple's Standard EULA covers it:

https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

Leave the License Agreement field in App Store Connect on the default. Revisit this if the app gains accounts, purchases or sharing.
