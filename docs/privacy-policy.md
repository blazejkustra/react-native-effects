# RN Effects Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-30

RN Effects is a free iPhone app that shows interactive visual effects. It is published by Blazej Kustra, an individual developer ("I" in this policy). This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## Summary

RN Effects has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting SDK. I do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal data. Your photos, audio and sensor readings stay on your iPhone.

## Permissions the app asks for

You can change each of these at any time in the iOS Settings app. Refusing a permission only affects the effects that use it.

### Microphone

The Dandelion and Birthday Candle effects use the microphone so you can blow on the screen. The AI Orb and AI Voice effects use it when you tap to start listening, so the visuals follow your voice.

The app reads the sound level and frequency of the live signal to drive the animation. Audio is never recorded, saved or transmitted. The microphone stops when you stop listening or leave the effect.

### Camera

The Polaroid effect uses the back camera so you can take a photo and watch it develop as an instant print. You have to allow the camera and press the shutter.

The photo is held in the app's temporary storage on your iPhone so it can be shown on screen. It is never uploaded and is never added to your photo library. iOS clears this temporary storage over time, and deleting the app removes it.

### Motion and magnetic sensors

Many effects use the accelerometer and device motion so they respond when you tilt, shake or flip your iPhone. The Ferrofluid effect uses the magnetometer so it responds to a magnet held near the phone. Readings are used live for the animation, and are never stored or transmitted.

## On-screen snapshots

Some effects take a picture of their own screen content to animate it, for example a chat bubble that turns to dust. These images are kept in temporary storage on your iPhone while the animation runs and are never transmitted.

## Network use

The app works without an account and sends none of your data to me. There is one network feature:

- The Voice Delete effect downloads four placeholder pictures from the public image service Lorem Picsum (picsum.photos). As with any web request, that service and its hosting providers receive your IP address and basic request details. I do not receive this information and the request contains nothing else about you. Lorem Picsum's own terms and privacy practices apply to it.

Links on the About screen open in your browser, where the website's own privacy policy applies.

## Apple

Apple distributes the app. Apple may give me aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports if you agreed to share them with app developers in your iOS settings. These reports do not identify you to me. Apple's privacy policy applies to that processing.

## Data retention and deletion

I hold no data about you, so there is nothing for me to retain or delete. To remove everything the app has stored on your iPhone, delete the app. To withdraw a permission, turn it off in the iOS Settings app.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR give you rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion. Since I hold no personal data through the app, a request will usually have nothing to act on, but you are welcome to contact me. If you email me, I use your message and address only to reply, and I delete them when the conversation is finished. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, I will update this page and its effective date before releasing that version.
